# 🎯 Atlas — AI Interview Platform

**Atlas** is a full-stack **AI-powered interview and recruitment platform** designed for business owners, HR professionals, recruiters, and interviewers.

The platform allows organizations to create their own customized **AI interviewer** by providing a job description, rules, instructions, required skills, and evaluation criteria. The AI interviewer can then conduct interviews, analyze candidate performance, and provide AI-assisted feedback and hiring recommendations.

Atlas combines **Frontend Development, Backend Development, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Model Training** into a single recruitment platform.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🤖 Custom AI Interviewer

HR professionals and interviewers can create customized AI interviewers by providing:

* Job Description
* Required Skills
* Interview Rules
* Custom Instructions
* Evaluation Criteria
* Interview Questions
* Candidate Requirements

The AI interviewer uses these instructions to conduct interviews according to the specific job requirements.

### 👤 Candidate Management

Recruiters can:

* Add and manage candidates
* Upload candidate CVs
* View candidate profiles
* Track interview status
* Review candidate performance
* Access interview history

### 📄 CV Analysis

Candidates can provide their CV/resume, which can be analyzed alongside the job requirements.

The system can help identify:

* Relevant skills
* Experience
* Qualifications
* Job compatibility
* Missing requirements

### 🎥 Interview Recording

The platform supports interview recording so HR professionals can review candidate interviews.

Recruiters can access the recorded interview and evaluate the candidate's performance.

### 🧠 AI-Based Candidate Evaluation

Atlas uses AI/ML techniques to analyze candidate information and interview performance.

The system can generate:

* Performance feedback
* Skill assessment
* Strengths and weaknesses
* Interview analysis
* Overall candidate evaluation
* Hiring recommendation

Possible recommendations include:

```text
✅ Accept / Shortlist
⚠️ Further Review
❌ Reject
```

The AI recommendation is intended as **decision support**, while the final hiring decision remains with the responsible human interviewer or organization.
